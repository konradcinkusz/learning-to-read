\section*{The year in this book}

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}c c c >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{27mm} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Part} & \textbf{Days} & \textbf{Season} & \textbf{What you read} & \textbf{The sounds} \\
\midrule
1 & 1--65    & autumn & 1 word a day     & One letter, one sound: \textit{van, mum, Pip, bag}. From week 6,
double letters and the last letters: \textit{duck, bell, box, yes, quack}. Words of three
sounds at most, and never two consonants together. \\
2 & 66--130  & winter & 2 words a day    & Two or three letters for one sound: \textit{ship, rain, feet, night,
boat, moon, car, cow}. Words of up to four sounds. \\
3 & 131--195 & spring & 3 words a day    & New ways to write the same sounds (\textit{day, bird, blue, cloud}),
the magic e (\textit{cake, bike, bone}), and two consonants together
(\textit{nest, jump}). Words of up to five sounds. On Fridays, a few
tricky words (\textit{the, said, you}), read whole. \\
4 & 196--260 & summer & 1 sentence a day & No more single words: a short sentence, from two words to four,
made only of words that can be read sound by sound, the tricky words of
the spring and the names of the story. It is the bridge to
\textit{Read and Draw}. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\vspace{6mm}
Every part has 13 weeks of 5 days -- holidays too: one page for every
weekday, at Christmas and in the summer as well as in term time. Every
week tells a little of the story of Lucía, her brother Dani, their dog
Toby and the new neighbours from London, with the same weekly themes as
\textit{Read and Draw}: if two children at home have one book each, that
week they read about the same thing.

\vspace{4mm}
The names of the story that can't be read sound by sound yet --
\textbf{Lucía}, \textbf{Toby}, \textbf{Amy}, \textbf{Dani},
\textbf{Grandma}... -- are read whole, like your own name, from the first
day. Their cards have no buttons.

\vspace{4mm}
There is a medal at the end of each part, and at the end of the book a
diploma and an \textbf{answer key} (for \lblAdivina, \lblUne,
\lblEncuentra, \lblPalmadas\ and \lblSiNo), in case something needs
checking.
